Program Questions

What to know before you apply.

Athletes and serious lifters who already train consistently and want structured, tested programming rather than open gym access. Most applicants are coming from 6+ months of regular training.

Each session caps at six athletes per coach, so every set gets watched and corrected in real time — closer to small-group personal training than a typical class.

You'll get a full results review — strength testing, body composition scan and a recommendation for your next cycle or track.

Track changes are reviewed at the end of each 6-week cycle to keep programming consistent, but your coach can flag an earlier switch if it's clearly the better fit.

Spots for the next cycle are limited.
